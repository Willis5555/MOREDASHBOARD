Lock for 1 to 5,555 days. Unstaking before the unlock date costs a penalty that grows the less time you served. There's a 14-day grace period after unlock, then a late penalty starts, scaling over 700 days. Every penalty is recycled into the reward pool for stakers still in — which is where all rewards come from; there's no fixed rate. Staking takes two transactions: one to approve the amount, one to open the stake.
